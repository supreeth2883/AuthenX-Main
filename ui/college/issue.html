🔐 Double-Signing Model
1
Issuance Signature — Generated now by your connector via HSM over the canonical hash. Stored alongside the hash in AuthenX's database.
2
Live Verification Signature — Generated at every employer verification. Connector re-fetches ERP data, rebuilds canonical JSON, re-signs with a fresh nonce. This ensures the credential is still valid in your ERP at the moment of verification.
Both signatures must match for a credential to be VERIFIED. A mismatch (e.g. student data changed in ERP) returns MISMATCH.
